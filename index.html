04 · WHY IT MATTERS

Why Khadi matters.

Better choices begin with understanding.

Khadi carries more than texture — it carries the skill of hand-spinning, hand-weaving and generations of textile craftsmanship.

Its production can support traditional skills and rely less on mechanised processes. But a sneaker’s impact goes beyond its fabric: every material, every process and every step of its lifespan matters.

Because what you wear matters — and knowing its story matters even more.
